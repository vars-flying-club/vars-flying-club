Why join VARS?

  • A small club with a family feel
  • Our plane is kept in a hangar
  • While we only have one plane, the airplane’s utilization is very, very low, making it easy to schedule a trip
  • Our hourly fee is by far the lowest for a high-performance airplane in Arizona, and it is a tach rate, not Hobbs
  • The airplane is one of the most capable club airplanes in the state in many ways — a very high useful load, excellent high-density-altitude performance, and 135 knots cruise at 11 to 12 gallons per hour
  • Our monthly dues are about half the cost of other clubs
  • Online scheduling and 24-hour access

We take pride in our aircraft, and we love to fly. If you are looking for a place to fly at reasonable cost and to be part of a good group of pilots, fill out the questionnaire above and we will get back to you. For anything else, the Contact page will reach us.

Membership

  • $100 processing fee — non-refundable (it goes towards updating our Arizona Corporation Commission documentation and paying for a new Falcon Field gate key)
  • $2,000 to join
  • $100 per month dues — paid quarterly in advance, $300 every three months (these cover our fixed costs: insurance to cover our member pilots, the hangar, registration and expected annual charges)
  • $60 per hour, dry — on tach time (this goes towards maintenance expenses and engine reserves)
  • Payment — by Zelle only

Members are responsible for fueling the aircraft after use. It burns roughly 10.5 to 13 gallons per hour of tach time at a cruise power setting.

Our fixed expenses are covered by the low monthly dues, and the expenses related to actually flying the aircraft are factored into the low hourly rate.

Before you apply, it is worth reading how we run — we are not a rental operation, and every member is expected to contribute something to the club.
