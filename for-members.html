Dues and fees

Processing fee $100 one-time Non-refundable (it goes towards updating our Arizona Corporation Commission documentation and paying for a new Falcon Field gate key).
Membership fee $2,000 one-time
Dues — monthly $100 per month
Dues — quarterly $300 every three months
Hourly rate $60 per hour, dry Charged on tach time, and goes towards maintenance expenses and engine reserves. Members fuel the aircraft after use.

Payments are accepted by Zelle only.

For payment arrangements and anything else to do with dues, contact the club treasurer.
