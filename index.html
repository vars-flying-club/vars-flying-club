Valley Aircraft Restoration Society

Welcome

We are a flying club at Falcon Field in Mesa, Arizona, sharing a four-seat, IFR-equipped 1972 Piper Cherokee 235F kept in a locked hangar.

The club began in 1989 as a group of volunteers restoring helicopters for static display, and became a flying club in the late 1990s. The Cherokee has been ours since 2017. The full story is on the History page.

We are small by design — membership is capped at fifteen — and we meet monthly to talk about trips, the aircraft, and maintenance.

  • Home field — Falcon Field, Mesa, Arizona (KFFZ)
  • Aircraft — 1972 Piper Cherokee 235F, N2653T — four seats, IFR equipped
  • Rate — $60 per hour, dry, on tach time
  • Dues — $100 per month, paid quarterly
  • Membership — capped at 15 pilots
  • Scheduling — online through AircraftClubs, with 24-hour access

Interested in joining? Our membership is currently full, but we still accept applications — see the Join page.
