About Us

Located at Falcon Field in Mesa, Arizona. VARS is non-profit flying club.

  • Our current aircraft is a four seat IFR equipped 1972 Piper Cherokee 235F.
  • Our costs are based off Tach time use, dry.
  • We keep our aircraft in a locked hangar
  • Our club has a cap of 15 members.
  • We meet monthly to discuss trips, the aircraft, our spouses, and maintenance. Not necessarily in that order.
  • We use an online scheduling system
  • There is no minimums on use if you want to take the aircraft overnight, or for extended trips.
